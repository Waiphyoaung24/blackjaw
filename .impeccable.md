## Design Context

### Users

Buyers, distributors, and partners evaluating Red Horse Industries — a Myanmar manufacturing, distribution, and nourishment group. Visitors arrive cold from search, referrals, or a partner deck. The job-to-be-done is _trust assessment in under 30 seconds_: confirm scale, legitimacy, and reach before opening a partnership conversation. Mobile-first browsing dominates in-region; desktop dominates international due diligence.

### Brand Personality

**Generous, trustworthy, human.** Three-word personality: _warm, confident, plainspoken._ The interface should evoke the calm assurance of a category-leading consumer brand — not the cold authority of a holding-company PDF. Photography and whitespace carry weight; type stays modest.

### Aesthetic Direction

A high-contrast monochrome system (white canvas, near-black ink) with a single warm yellow voltage carrying primary action. Structurally borrows from Airbnb's modest-type / soft-shape / single-voltage discipline, but the palette is its own — black + white + yellow, not Rausch red.

- **Canvas:** Pure white `#ffffff`. No paper tones, no grain overlay, no warm background.
- **Voltage:** A single yellow — `#f7bd02` — carries every primary CTA and brand mark. Used scarcely; ~90% of the page is white + ink. Because yellow has low contrast against white, **CTA labels render in ink (`#282828`), never white.**
- **Ink:** `#282828` (deep near-black, never pure `#000`). Body text at `#3f3f3f`, muted at `#6a6a6a`, hairlines at `#dddddd`.
- **Type:** Modest weights everywhere — display at 500–700, body at 400. Photography carries hierarchy, not typographic muscle.
- **Shape:** Soft everywhere. Radius ladder is fixed: `xs 4 / sm 8 / md 14 / lg 20 / xl 32 / full 9999`. No ad-hoc 6px or 22px.
- **Elevation:** One shadow tier only — `rgba(0,0,0,0.02) 0 0 0 1px, rgba(0,0,0,0.04) 0 2px 6px, rgba(0,0,0,0.1) 0 4px 8px`. Flat otherwise.
- **Spacing:** 4px base, 64px section padding.

**Anti-references:**

- Editorial / magazine layouts (Mirage, Pentagram studio sites).
- Heavy serif display typography (Fraunces at hero scale).
- Paper / cream backgrounds with grain noise.
- Glassmorphism nav bars over photo backgrounds.
- Dual-color CTA systems (e.g. yellow pill + red dot).
- Pure black (`#000`) anywhere — always `#282828`.
- White text on yellow (contrast fail).

### Design Principles

1. **One voltage.** Yellow `#f7bd02` carries primary CTAs and the brand mark — nothing else. If two yellows of different shades appear on screen, one is wrong.
2. **Ink on yellow, never white.** Primary CTA labels are `#282828`. White-on-yellow fails contrast and looks washed.
3. **Modest type, loud photography.** Display weights stay at 500–700 and never feel like they're shouting. Visual hierarchy comes from images and whitespace. _Exception: the BLACKJAW wordmark and full-bleed hero headings retain heavy display weight (Montserrat 800–900) as a brand signature — body, section headings, captions, and nav stay 500–700._
4. **Soft, never sharp.** Every interactive element is rounded — pills for search/CTAs, `rounded.md` for cards, `rounded.full` for icons. The body grid is the only hard edge.
5. **One shadow tier or none.** Depth comes from white-on-white separation and rounded-corner clipping, not layered elevation.
6. **Touch targets are non-negotiable.** Primary actions ≥ 48×48px; secondary ≥ 44×44px. This applies to nav and footer links, not just CTAs.
7. **Snap to tokens.** Radii, spacing, type sizes, and colors all reference `DESIGN.md` tokens. No bespoke per-component values.
